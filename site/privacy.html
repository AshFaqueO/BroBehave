BroBehave

Privacy policy

Draft — last updated 13 November 2026. This page mirrors PRIVACY.md in the project.

BroBehave is a social accountability app for adults (18+). It deals with a sensitive subject, so it is built to collect as little as possible. There are no ads, no advertising identifiers, no analytics trackers, and your data is never sold.

What we never collect

  • The names of websites you visit, how many times, or at what time.
  • Your private notes on a relapse or slip. They stay on your phone and are never uploaded.
  • Your contacts, location, photos, or browsing history.

Automatic detection (optional, off by default)

If you turn on automatic detection, BroBehave uses Android's VPN feature on your phone only:

  • What is checked: only the names of websites your phone looks up, compared on the phone against a list of adult sites. Nothing is blocked or changed, and your traffic is not routed through any BroBehave server.
  • What is shared: only that a day was flagged, which lowers your rank for 7 days.
  • What is never shared or stored: which sites, how many times, or when. Site names are discarded the moment they are checked, even on your phone.

You see a full explanation and must agree before it starts, and you can turn it off at any time from the app or its notification.

Where your data is stored

Account and app data is stored with Supabase (database, sign-in and file storage). Push notifications are delivered through Firebase Cloud Messaging and crash reports through Firebase Crashlytics, both by Google. Data is encrypted in transit.

Your choices

  • Choose what friends see (streak, relapses, rank) in Settings → Privacy.
  • Turn each notification type on or off in Settings.
  • Block or report anyone; remove friends at any time.
  • Delete your account and all its data in Settings → Delete my account, or through the account deletion page.

A flagged day cannot be removed once recorded; this is what keeps ranks honest. Deleting your account removes them along with everything else.

Age

BroBehave is for adults only. You must confirm you are 18 or older to use it. We do not knowingly keep data about anyone under 18.
