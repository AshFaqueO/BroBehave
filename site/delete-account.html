Delete your BroBehave account

You can delete your account and all of its data at any time. Deletion is permanent and cannot be undone.

In the app (immediate)

  1. Open BroBehave and go to Profile.
  2. Tap Settings.
  3. Scroll to Account and tap Delete my account.
  4. Confirm with Delete forever.

Without the app

If you no longer have the app, email [contact email to be added] from the address you signed up with, with the subject “Delete my BroBehave account”. We delete the account within 30 days and confirm by email.

What is deleted

  • Your sign-in (email) and profile: username, display name, avatar.
  • Your streak, longest streak, rank, relapse dates and flagged days.
  • Your posts, your reactions, and reactions on your posts.
  • Your friendships, friend requests, blocks, and notification settings and tokens.

What is kept

Nothing that identifies you. Reports you filed about other people are deleted with your account. Crash reports already sent do not contain your identity and expire on their own (90 days).
