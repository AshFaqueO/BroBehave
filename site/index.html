BroBehave

Bro, Behave.

BroBehave is an Android app for quitting together. Track your streak, add your bros, see each other's ranks, cheer the wins and laugh off the slips. It is for adults (18+) only.

What it does

  • Streak: days since your last relapse.
  • Ranks: nine steps from Clown to Giga Chad, earned by streak days.
  • Friends: a feed of your bros' rank-ups and resets, with reactions.
  • Your privacy: you choose what friends see. No ads, no trackers, nothing sold.
